# Data platform architecture

## Overview

The retail_Innovate_ai_test repository models a retail and supply chain data environment designed for multi-channel analytics and AI-assisted review. It includes raw extract ingestion, conformed data standards, and curated KPI tables for operational decision-making.

## Layering model

### Bronze

The Bronze layer captures raw operational extracts from the following functional domains:

- point-of-sale transactions
- inventory snapshots
- warehouse movement logs
- supplier shipments
- return and fulfillment exception events

These are stored as source-aligned tables without assuming full business semantics.

### Silver

The Silver layer normalizes these feeds with datatypes, deduplication, business keys, and quality checks. Typical improvements include:

- standardized date parsing
- null resolution for critical dimensions
- key alignment across stores, SKUs, and suppliers
- conformance to retail and fulfillment semantics

### Gold

The Gold layer supports data product consumption. It provides curated views for:

- inventory health
- supplier performance
- service level exceptions
- return analysis and cause clustering

## AI audit angle

The project includes a lightweight AI audit pattern intended to produce richer narrative insights for governance committees and executive review boards. This is not a replacement for formal risk controls but serves as an explainable layer that surfaces operational concerns and recommended remediation.

Common AI-auditor outputs:

- service risk from low inventory coverage
- elevated carrier or warehouse delay risk
- supplier quality degradation
- returns volatility and post-purchase friction

## Decision support

This architecture is designed to enable the orchestration of:

- downstream dashboards
- feature engineering for forecasting
- AI recommendation services
- governance narratives for internal audit and business review
